# Barbed wire in war

In the First World War, belts of barbed wire in front of trenches slowed infantry advances under machine-gun fire. Armies cut the wire with shears and bombarded it with artillery, and later used tanks to crush it.

During the Boer War, the British linked lines of blockhouses with barbed wire fences to restrict the movement of Boer fighters across the veldt.

The Berlin Wall began on 13 August 1961 as barbed wire and fencing strung across the city, and was later rebuilt in concrete.
