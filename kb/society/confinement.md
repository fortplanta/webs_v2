# Barbed wire and confinement

Prisons and detention facilities adopted barbed wire for perimeter security, and later razor wire, because it is cheap, quick to put up, and hard to climb.

Camps in the twentieth century, including the Nazi concentration camps, were enclosed by barbed wire fences, which is one reason the material came to stand for confinement and the loss of freedom.

Modern border barriers often use coils of razor wire. Hungary built such a fence along its border with Serbia in 2015 during the migrant crisis.

On farms, wire made it possible to separate livestock by field, which supported controlled grazing and breeding.
