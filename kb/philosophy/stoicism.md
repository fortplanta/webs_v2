# Stoicism

Stoicism was founded by Zeno of Citium in Athens around 300 BCE. It teaches that virtue is the only good and that we should focus on what is within our control.

Epictetus, a former slave, taught that people are disturbed not by things but by their judgments about things. His student Arrian recorded the Discourses and the Enchiridion.

Marcus Aurelius, Roman emperor from 161 to 180 CE, wrote the Meditations as private notes on practicing Stoic discipline during war and plague.

Modern cognitive behavioral therapy draws explicitly on Stoic ideas: Aaron Beck and Albert Ellis both credited Epictetus as an influence on the idea that beliefs shape emotion.
