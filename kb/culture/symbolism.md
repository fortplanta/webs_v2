# Barbed wire as a symbol

Amnesty International's logo, designed in 1963 by Diana Redhouse, shows a candle wrapped in barbed wire. The wire represents imprisonment and oppression, and the candle stands for hope.

Farmers and cowboys nicknamed barbed wire the "devil's rope". The name reflects how strongly people reacted to a fence that could injure animals and people.

Some historians describe barbed wire as a symbol of dispossession, because it turned shared land into private property and shut out people who had used it for generations. Others emphasise how it made farming on the plains possible.
