hint: The one constraint that matters, and how to bust it
---
Zoom out and spot the one thing that's keeping the selected system, process or situation from running at full tilt. Don't get distracted by tweaks or marginal gains; go for the jugular.

What's the constraint? Then give the wild, counterintuitive move that could actually bust it open, plus a quick way to test whether you've found the real bottleneck.
