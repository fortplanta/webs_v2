---
id: assumption-audit
name: Assumption audit
mode: shift
hint: Find the hidden bets and what happens when they fail
---
Take the selected content (a strategy, plan, claim or idea) apart and hunt for the hidden bets it's making.

For each hidden assumption:
- State it plainly.
- Ask: what if reality doesn't play along?
- Map the knock-on effects, the risks, and any unexpected upsides that might pop up when the assumption doesn't hold.

Rank the assumptions by how much of the plan rests on them and how shaky they are. Finish with the two or three that most deserve a cheap test, and what that test would be.
