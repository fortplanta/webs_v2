Map the space of possible plays. From the Decision Brief, the bottleneck and the Pattern Table, build an Options Table of two to five coherent options, each explicitly targeting the bottleneck or a key leverage point.

Each row: a short label and description; how it targets the bottleneck and which patterns it addresses; expected upside (against the success criteria); expected downside and trade-offs; reversibility; rough speed and cost (fast or slow, light or heavy); and fit with the existing system (friction versus harmony).
