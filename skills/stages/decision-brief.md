---
Turn the diagnosis into a crisp Decision Brief, so it's clear what is actually being decided. No new facts; reframe the diagnosis as a decision.
- Decision statement: one crisp question (for example "Which of these three directions do we ship, given constraint X?" or "How should ownership of Y be structured to remove bottleneck Z?").
- Context: three to five sentences derived purely from the diagnosis.
- Decision owner and key actors, mapped from the Situation Card (who says yes or no, who must be consulted, who just needs to be informed).
- Success criteria: what "good" looks like, few and testable, aligned with the outcome at stake and the system as it really runs.
- Constraints: hard versus soft.
