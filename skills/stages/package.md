Assemble the final Decision Package, something a human can actually use.
1. Executive summary: the constraint (the bottleneck in one line), the decisive move (the chosen option or portfolio), and the expected impact against the success criteria with a timeframe.
2. Prioritised action plan, five to seven steps. For each: the action, the owner (by role), timing, the gate (what condition moves you forward or pivots) and the linked loop.
3. The top slice of the risk and assumption register: highest-impact, highest-uncertainty bets, with tests and mitigations.
4. Metrics and feedback loops: the dials you will actually watch, with cadence and triggers.
5. Options and hedges: how backup options come into play under which signals.
6. Open unknowns and tensions that weren't resolved.

Reuse what the earlier stages produced; don't re-derive it. Keep it tight.
