Name the throat of the hourglass. Using the Situation Card, System Snapshot and Pattern Table:
- Select one binding constraint: the narrowest point limiting the desired outcome. State it in one line.
- Design a leveraged intervention: one to three actions that directly hit that constraint.
- Anticipate second-order effects and watch-outs.
- Define a simple validation test: metric, threshold and timeframe.

Close with a compact Diagnostic Report: the bottleneck, the suggested moves and the validation test, in a form that could be handed to someone else.
