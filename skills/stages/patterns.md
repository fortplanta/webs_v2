Flip on the blacklight. Scan the Situation Card and System Snapshot for:
- Patterns and anti-patterns (no owner, too many reviewers, vague goals, misaligned incentives).
- Conflicting goals (brand versus performance, speed versus quality, safety versus experimentation).
- Repeated failure modes (last-minute changes, zombie projects, burnout cycles).
- Missed opportunities: clear upside, low cost, untouched.

Output a Pattern Table where each row has: the pattern, anti-pattern or opportunity; the evidence from the input and the snapshot; the likely cause; and a possible safeguard, or a way to exploit or fix it.
