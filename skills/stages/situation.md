Turn the selected content, whatever it is (text, an image description, a link, a described conflict or decision, or a mix), into a Situation Card v1. First detect what kind of input it is. Then extract and infer, clearly marking what is inferred rather than stated:
- Topic or domain: what this seems to be about.
- Main actors: people, audiences, platforms.
- Outcome at stake: what "good" would look like.
- What's currently happening: observed behaviour, artefact or conflict.
- Explicit constraints (deadlines, budgets, rules), if visible.
- Strong emotions or tensions (anger, confusion, pressure, fear).

Everything later hangs off this card, so keep it compact and exact. No analysis yet.
